a. Some pros of Agile development is that it has an iterative approach and constantly adds value to the customer. Some cons of Agile development is that it is hard to predict the budget and timeline of the project and can make collaboration difficult.
b. The Scrum Master completes all the scrum related tasks, for example, creating sprints and meetings. The Product Owner sets the direction for the team and handles things such as stakeholder management.
c. The three types of meetings that occur during a sprint are the sprint planning meeting, the daily scrum/standup meeting, and the sprint retrospective meeting. The planning meeting is for determining what will be completed in the following sprint and oving stories from the backlog into the current sprint. The daily scrum/standup meeting is for discussing the work they already completed and planning the work for that day. The retrospective meeting is for discussing completed work and moving anything not finished into the backlog.
